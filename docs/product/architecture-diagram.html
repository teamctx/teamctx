teamctx

A deterministic, read-only cross-agent context broker — the credibility engine that tells an agent, before it acts, whether the shared context it is relying on is still trustworthy. It emits typed, witnessed cards plus an honest coverage certificate, and refuses to adjudicate. No model sits in the trusted path.

Product/Value · Positioning

Agents broke the speed at which work moves. Team coordination didn't keep up.

Timely, ambient team context.

Humans and their agents, working from the same live picture of the current state of the work — and the standards around it. The movement in your PRs, your tickets, your docs, surfaced where the work happens, the moment it's relevant.

Context, not memory·delivered, not stored·the facts already live in your tools — teamctx transforms the delivery, not the content
What it's for
Fewer collisions.
Less rework.
  • The standards around the work, actually adhered to
  • Faster ramp onto unfamiliar code, faster resumption after a break
  • Cross-agent continuity — swap Claude Code, Cursor, Codex freely; context is just data
  • Fewer stale-spec and missed-gate mistakes

It informs; it doesn't enforce. The benefit is better-informed work when the team acts on it — never a guaranteed outcome. And the absence of a card is never an all-clear: teamctx always tells you what it couldn't check.

Illustrative — the target experience. One glance before the agent acts: a PR you'd forgotten and a ticket whose criteria moved — without leaving the editor or asking "go check all my PRs and issues." GitHub and repo docs are live today; Jira, Confluence and the rest are on the roadmap (see the source strip below, and the Engineering tab).

The because — the credibility engine

Why a team can trust the picture.

The differentiator under the category: no LLM in the content path. Facts arrive verbatim, source-backed, one-click verifiable, and replayable — teamctx is the deterministic ground an agent can stand on and check itself against. Complementary to the LLM, never adversarial: the model does judgment and generation, teamctx hands it trustworthy current facts. The same construction means it can't track you and can't be hijacked by a poisoned artifact.
